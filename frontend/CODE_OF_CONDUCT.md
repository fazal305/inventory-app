# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **fazalabbas2002@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently for repeated or serious violations.
